(Remarks to the Author) 
This manuscript entitled “Orbital exchange-mediated current control of magnetism” presents a novel and comprehensive
theoretical framework for current-induced control of magnetism via orbital exchange interactions, extending beyond the
conventional spin-based mechanisms. The orbital degrees of freedom including the reduced orbital angular momentum
(OAM) and orbital angular position (OAP) are included in the analysis of current induced magnetic effects. These results not
only provide new physical insights for the role of orbital dynamics in magnetism, but also provide experimentally verifiable
predictions, which will be of great significance to the wider field of spintronics and condensed matter physics.

In the submitted manuscript, the authors discuss the contribution of orbital exchange to current-induced control of
magnetism. They consider a simple model with three orbitals and spin–orbit interactions, and derive the correction arising
