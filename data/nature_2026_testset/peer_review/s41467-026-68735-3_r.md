Reviewer comments: 
Reviewer #1 
(Remarks to the Author) 
In my opinion, the authors have addressed the main concerns raised by Reviewer #3 with additional measurements, the
revised manuscript can be accepted. 
The authors have addressed my previous concerns carefully. The scientific validity and clarity of the manuscript have been
improved. I would recommend the acceptance. 
Reviewer #4 
(Remarks to the Author) 
Addition: 
I read the comments of the other reviewer and the replies from the authors. 
I would say that the authors responded
properly. I thus recommend acceptance of this manuscript. 
The authors have substantially improved the manuscript in response to the earlier review comments. The revised version
now presents a clearer scientific argument, and the integration of machine learning screening with DFT validation and
experimental insight is communicated more convincingly. 
Most of the earlier technical concerns have now been addressed. The machine learning workflow is better documented, the
benchmark comparisons are appropriate, and validation against DFT is clearly shown in the updated Supplementary parity
plots. However, a few final clarifications would improve transparency and reproducibility: 
The manuscript should explicitly report the Hubbard U values used for Ni, Fe and W in the DFT calculations, preferably in
both the Methods and Supplementary Information. Since U values affect reaction energetics, it would be helpful for the
authors to briefly state whether small variations in U impact the main trends and conclusions. 
Additionally, since Fe impurities in KOH electrolytes are known to artificially enhance OER activity in Ni-based catalysts, a
short statement confirming whether Fe-free KOH was used, or whether Fe contamination was monitored or controlled, would
strengthen the reliability of the electrochemical results. 
Regarding data availability: the authors indicate that machine learning training configurations, checkpoints and structures
will be shared after publication. This is acceptable, but it would be helpful to clarify in the Data Availability section that these
materials will be deposited in a stable repository and accessible upon publication, as per journal policy. 
These are minor but important additions that can be addressed easily without additional experiments. Once incorporated,
the manuscript will meet the standards of methodological clarity and reproducibility expected in this field. I support
publication after these minor revisions. No need for further review.
