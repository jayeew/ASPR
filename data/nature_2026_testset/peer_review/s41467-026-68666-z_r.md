Specific Comments R2 -4: Calculations on the reaction pathway to elucidate the high 
intrinsic activity in C -N coupling and selective glycine production are suggested to be 
conducted. 
Response: We acknowledge the helpful suggestion of the reviewer. We conducted the key  
elementary step of glycine production on Pd SA/BCNx and TiO 2, namely the C -N coupling 
between *CH2OHCHO and *NH 2 (Nat. Commun., 2025, 16, 8040)  (Figure S23d ) in the 
manuscript. *CH2OHCHO and *NH 2 species are adsorbed on a single Pd site on Pd 
SA/BCNx and associated to the *CH 2OHCHONH2 with an activation barrier of 0.18 eV . In 
contrast, the *CH 2OHCHO and *NH 2 species are adsorbed on two neighboring Ti sites on 
TiO2, requiring 0.47 eV to activate the C−N bond formation. Meanwhile, the active lattice 
oxygen on TiO2 is known to oxidize the adsorbed species, such as the −C=O or −CHO group 
(Figure S23e ) (Nat. Commun., 2024, 15, 6827) . Therefore, compared with TiO 2, the Pd 
SA/BCNx catalyst preferred the C−N coupling step to further produce the glycine. These 
results provide a rational theoretical explanation for the high selectivity of Pd SA/BCNx for 
glycine production.
