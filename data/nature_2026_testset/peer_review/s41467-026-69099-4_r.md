AUTHOR RESPONSE TO REVIEWER COMMENTS 
Reviewer #1 (Remarks to the Author): 
The authors properly and professionally addressed all of my original concerns. 
Response: We thank the reviewer for the positive comment
Reviewer #2 (Remarks to the Author): 
The authors have provided a substantial amount of additional data, which adequately addresses all my 
concerns. The manuscript now demonstrates sufficient novelty and robust data integrity, meeting the 
standards for publication in Nature Communications. 
Response: We thank the reviewer for the positive comment
Reviewer #3 (Remarks to the Author): 
To better evaluate the generalizability of the LBD-CID strategy, it would be interested to test the effects of 
antagonists on RARg and VDR, as such molecules are available. However, I consider that authors have 
answered the concerns raised by reviewer #3. 
Response: We thank the reviewer for the positive comment and the suggestion. We agree that testing RARg 
and VDR antagonists could further inform the generalizability of the LBD-CID strategy and represent a 
direction for future studies. Here, however, we deliberately focused on ligand–receptor pairs for which 
clinically approved compounds are available. To the best of our knowledge, no clinically approved 
antagonists currently exist for RARg or VDR, but we will certainly keep your advice in mind for our future 
work.
