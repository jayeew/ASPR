Reviewer comments: 
Reviewer #1 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have adequately addressed the major concerns raised in the initial review. Figure labeling, legends, and
methodological descriptions have been substantially improved, and key control experiments have been appropriately
included or clarified. The revised data presentation is clearer and more internally consistent, particularly in the corrected
Figure 7 panels and updated schematic. Overall, the manuscript has significantly improved in both scientific rigor and clarity.
I have no further major concerns and support consideration of the manuscript in its current form. 
Reviewer #2 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have done an excellent job of addressing my comments and critiques. 
Reviewer #3 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have submitted an extensive revised manuscript that incorporated the major criticisms, through additional
experiments, further providing quantified data and text revisions. To my opinion, the revision is done very meticulously and
detailed providing further support for their conclusions on the role of TMEM110 in maintaining lysosomal homeostasis. 
I am fully satisfied about how the authors addressed my previous concerns and have no further comments.
