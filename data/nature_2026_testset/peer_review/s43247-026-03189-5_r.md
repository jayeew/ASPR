Reviewer #3 (Comments for the Author):
Dear Rachael Treharne and co-authors,
Thank you very much for your extensive work in responding to all reviewers concerns, and in 
particular I agree with Table S5. However, most of my major concerns could not be clarified 
or resolved and hence I cannot accept this manuscript for publication.
Previous points 1 and 2) Turestky et al prescribe abrupt thaw to follow a mean gradual thaw 
output of LSMs. I do not agree to use such assumption (speculation) as a basis of the analysis 
and conclusions in this study. Such relationship is unclear as gradual thawing and abrupt 
thawing processes occur under distinct environmental conditions and I do not see a clear 
relationship from first principles here, or any data-driven empirical relationship.
Previous point 6) This relationship is the basis for the estimation of fire-related C emissions.
There is no theoretical evidence for such linear relationship, and the presented data prove that 
there is not such relationship. I do not understand why you base your results and conclusions 
on such relationship. I cannot agree with that approach.
I am sorry that I cannot be more positive at this point as I find the topic very important and I 
would like to see new results published about abrupt thaw and fire effects on the carbonclimate 
feedback. However, the presented results and conclusions are too much based on 
assumptions that are not supported by theory or observations. Equations in simplified global 
models need to be supported either by observations (empirical) or by mechanistic model 
results (real Earth System Models) that are derived on first principles and validated on their
