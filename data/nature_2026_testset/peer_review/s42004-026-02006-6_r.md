Reviewer #2 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The revised manuscript has been substantially strengthened by the addition of thorough explanations and new experimental
data, which have helped me better understand the work. I appreciate the authors' efforts and am generally supportive of the
manuscript. Although it now appears suitable for publication, the authors may wish to address the following points: 
1. The reason why the isomer II does not contribute to the absorption spectrum at n = 13 and 14. Their contributions are not
negligible (especially for n = 13) in their photoelectron spectra. 
2. The specific structural motif of the isomer II. Is this another type of surface-bound states? I see that Ref. 26 did not show
the existence of the isomer II in QM/MM calculation. However, the authors in the revised manuscript have claimed that the
number of the established structural motifs of water cluster anions have now changed from 4 (Ia, Ib, II, and III) to 5 (Ia1, Ia2,
Ib, II, and III). Addressing this point would further improve the manuscript. 
3. What are "previously uncharacterized structural motifs" in Abstract and Conclusion sections? Are these Ia1 and Ia2? I
suppose that these have already been characterized by Refs. 26 and 28, as Ia1 = Partially embedded (the band I in Ref. 28)
and Ia2 = Proper surface (the band II in Ref. 28). Then, is the isomer II a previously uncharacterized one? The wordings are
a bit unclear for readers.

Reviewer #2: 
The revised manuscript has been substantially strengthened by the addition of thorough explanations and 
new experimental data, which have helped me better understand the work. I appreciate the authors' efforts 
and am generally supportive of the manuscript. Although it now appears suitable for publication, the 
authors may wish to address the following points: 
 
1. The reason why the isomer II does not contribute to the absorption spectrum at n = 13 and 14. Their 
contributions are not negligible (especially for n = 13) in their photoelectron spectra. 
We appreciate the reviewer ’s valuable comment . In particular, for n = 13 the contribution of 
isomer II may not be negligible, whereas it appears to be nearly absent for other clusters, 
including n = 14, under our experimental conditions. This was noted on page 7 as follows: 
Except for the n = 13 cluster, however, the contribution from II isomers is negligible. This observation 
strongly indicates that both experimentally observed absorption bands predominantly originate from I 
isomers across the entire cluster-size range investigated.  
2. The specific structural motif of the isomer II. Is this another type of surface-bound states? I see that Ref. 
26 did not show the existence of the isomer II in QM/MM calculation. However, the authors in the revised 
manuscript have claimed that the number of the established structural motifs of water cluster anions have 
now changed from 4 (Ia, Ib, II, and III) to 5 (Ia1, Ia2, Ib, II, and III). Addressing this point would further 
improve the manuscript. 
We agree that this point may be confusing t o readers. To clarify this issue, we have added the 
following statement on page 8:  
‘It should be emphasized again that the two surface -bound isomers predicted by QM/MM calculations 26 
should now be reassigned as Ia1 and Ia2, rather than the previously assi gned motifs Ia and II. The Ib and 
III isomers predicted by theory are consistent with the experimental observations. On the other hand, the 
experimentally identified II motif does not match the theoretical prediction. Therefore, although only four 
motifs were theoretically proposed, water anion clusters should be classified into five distinct motifs.” 
3. What are "previously uncharacterized structural motifs" in Abstract and Conclusion sections? Are these 
Ia1 and Ia2? I suppose that these have already been characterized by Refs. 26 and 28, as Ia1 = Partially 
embedded (the band I in Ref. 28) and Ia2 = Proper surface (the band II in Ref. 28). Then, is the isomer II a 
previously uncharacterized one? The wordings are a bit unclear for readers.

To reduce the amb iguity of the original phrasing, the relevant sentences have been revised as 
follows: 
Abstract: The structural motifs, previously characterized as four, are now established as five in the present 
work, thereby refining the framework of hydrated electron isomers. 
Conclusion: The four previously characterized isomeric structures are now expanded to five in the present 
work, thereby refining the framework of hydrated electron isomers.
