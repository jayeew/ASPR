Reviewer #1 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have carefully addressed the points raised by the reviewers but they have not increased the sample size. This
severely compromises the conclusions that can be drawn from their interesting observations. In the revised manuscript, the
authors have included a more detailed chapter on the study limitations. However, it would be important to explicitly state the
preliminary/pilot character in both the title and abstract.

We appreciate the reviewer's attention to appropriate framing of our findings. We respectfully note 
that the manuscript already extensively acknowledges the preliminary nature of this work and the 
constraints of our sample size. Specifically: 
 The Abstract states this is a study of "three ARIA+ patients and matched controls" 
 The Introduction explicitly frames this as a "case -control study of three sex, age, and 
APOE genotype matched pairs" 
 The Discussion opens by stating "the cross-sectional design precludes causal inference" 
and describes the work as "a hypothesis-generating resource" 
 The Discussion contains a dedicated limitations paragraph addressing sample size 
constraints, inability to assess APOE genotype-specific effects, and the need for validation 
in larger cohorts 
 The concluding sentence explicitly states these findings "establish a foundation for 
hypothesis testing in larger cohorts" 
We believe this level of qualification throughout the manuscript appropriately conveys the 
preliminary natur e of the work without requiring modification of the title, which we feel would 
understate the biological insights provided by our deep multi -omic characterization. We have, 
however, added "pending validation in larger cohorts" to the final sentence of the Abstract to 
further reinforce this point.
