Overall Comments 
REE mineralization process is a hot topic recently. This manuscript presents a study on high-temperature and pressure
experiments with natural observations, unequivocally identifying pressure (emplacement depth) as a primary controlling
factor for REE mineralization in carbonatites. Lots of factors controlling small fraction of global carbonatites hosting giant
REE deposits but were not clearly understood. The proposed "deep-seated enrichment vs. shallow-level dispersion" model
provides more illumination. Meanwhile, still some comments and questions need to be fixed. 
1. At first, I do not think the Abstract and Introduction have been well written as the novelty is not presented in good pattern
and nice logic. For examples, why pressure is a first-order control REE mineralization among many factors governing the
formation of giant deposits. 
2. In the Abstract, more explicitly state that this model revises current models that overemphasize hydrothermal fluid
immiscibility or apatite fractionation, establishing the primacy of pressure/depth. 
3. In the final paragraph of the Introduction (line ~67), specify "other, potentially more critical factors" as "emplacement depth
(pressure) and its control on the crystallization sequence and fluid/melt stability" to more clearly introduce the aim of this
study. 
4. This is the key problem of the ms. Fluent logic is lacking throughout the whole manuscript. The logical chain is largely
complete, but the connectivity of a few key links could be tighter. For examples, high-pressure apatite suppression
mechanism: The chain "early olivine crystallization-silica depletion-suppression of REE-rich apatite" relies on a critical
implicit assumption that silica activity is the primary limiting factor for REE-rich apatite crystallization. Is there some literature
or your data to support the view that "apatite crystallization in carbonatitic systems is strongly controlled by silica activity"
