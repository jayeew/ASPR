Reviewer #1 
(Remarks to the Author) 
This paper is now improved. 
A few minor only clarifications remain. 
1. The authors provide evidence now that the population of cells that they are calling Bregs throughout the manuscript is
actually a mixture of natural adipose Bregs, B1 cells and some plasma cells (though not age associated B cells). To avoid
confusion regarding the terminology of Bregs in the literature, I would recommend at least changing the use of the term Breg
in the title of the paper to something like, “Interleukin-10 expressing B lineage cells in visceral adipose tissue………”. 
The
mixed population of cells are most accurately called B-10 cells, or IL-10+IgM+ B cells. 
I would update the abstract as well
when introducing Bregs to avoid confusion with natural adipose Bregs which represent only a portion of the IL-10 cells
described. 
Can update the abstract to something like, “Here we report that IL-10+IgM+ B lineage cells (collectively referred to broadly as
regulatory B cells or “Bregs” here) are greatly expanded in age VAT ……….” 
Then you can keep your Breg terminology throughout the paper. 
The most accurate way would have been to call the cells as
B-10 cells or IL-10+IgM+ B cells throughout the entire paper to emphasize it is a mixed batch of cell types. 
Will leave these terminology changes to the discretion of editor and authors if they think it is necessary. 
2. Lines 258-273 seem to be duplicated in the manuscript?? I.e. the same paragraph is written twice? 
3. For completeness in their methods, can the authors clarify if their new Sup Fig 8K-M uses a Thr-308 Akt antibody at
baseline or after insulin challenge. The methods should also clearly state that they used a Thr-308 antibody on Akt, since
that one is more directly downstream of the Insulin receptor. 
In either case, it is probably ok since the more inflammatory B
cell IL-10 KO has less signaling, signifying more resistance in the pathway. 
Reviewer #2 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have addressed my previous concerns by building on the discussion, altering text and the addition of new
experiments. 
I have no additional comments.

mice without insulin or glucose stimulations and stored at -80°C until analysis.  Tissues were 54 
homogenized and lysed in RIPA buffer containing protease and phosphatase inhibitors. The protein 55 
lysates were separated by SDS -PAGE. After electrophoresis, the proteins were transferred to a 56 
polyvinylidene difluoride membrane (Millipore), incubated with blocking buffer for 1 h at room 57 
temperature, and then incubated with the following antibodies overnight at 4°C: anti-GAPDH, anti-58
