Reviewer #3 
(Remarks to the Author) 
The authors have provided detailed and thoughtful responses to the extensive comments from Reviewer 1. They have
addressed key points such as the limitations of ST analysis, the validity of RCTD, mIHC validation, the confusion between
LAR and TLS, issues with LAR/TLS annotation, grouping of spatial blocks, and the cross-section of slides. Each comment
was met with specific responses and improvements in the manuscript, except for the following points: 
1. The statement "provides several markers that could be predictive of immunotherapy response" in the newly added
abstract is an overstatement. As the study does not identify specific markers for immunotherapy, it would be more accurate to
conclude that the study provides new perspectives in the exploration of immunotherapy biomarkers. 
2. Regarding the definition of TLS and LAR in relation to the argument 2), the legends of Figures 2 and 3 still refer to TLS.
Even though HE staining is used for verification, it is difficult to conclusively demonstrate the presence of follicular structures
or HEVs indicative of true TLS. It would be more appropriate to revise these descriptions to LAR.
