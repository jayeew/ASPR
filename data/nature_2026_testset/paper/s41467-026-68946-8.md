Solar-driven atmospheric water harvesting (SAWH) holds signiﬁcant promise
for decentralized water supply. However, its widespread application is hin-
dered by two critical limitations: underutilization of high-humidity adsorption
windows during nighttime and insufﬁcient desorption during daytime due to
the high desorption temperature requirement of conventional sorbents. To
overcome these challenges, this study proposes a composite sorbent strategy
by synergistically combining the low enthalpy of vaporization of LiCl with the
robust adsorption capacity and stability of a metal‒organic framework (MOF,
speciﬁcally Ni
2Cl2(BTDD), H2BTDD = bis(1H−1,2,3-triazolo[4,5-b],[4′,5′-i])
dibenzo[1,4]dioxin). This design leverages the complementary properties to
achieve lower desorption temperatures (e.g., < 60
oC in device level) compared
to typical MOF-based systems (usually >90 oC in device level), thereby sig-
niﬁcantly reducing the energy consumption for desorption. Concurrently, the
composite exhibits extended adsorption duration within the high-humidity
window. Field validation across diverse climatic regions demonstrates the
composite’s exceptional wide-range environmental stability and performance.
The resulting SAWH device achieves a solar-to-water generation improvement
up to 91% in a continental ﬁeld test. This work presents a generalizable and
effective pathway for enhancing SAWH performance through synergistic
material engineering, enabling efﬁcient water production and thermal control
under varying environmental conditions.

Synthesis and characterizations of LiCl@Ni2Cl2(BTDD)
The composite adsorbent LiCl@Ni 2Cl2(BTDD)_x (x = 10/20/30/40,
representing different LiCl solutions) was prepared using a scalable
impregnation method in different mass concentrations (wt%) of LiCl

of Ni
2Cl2(BTDD) and LiCl@Ni 2Cl2(BTDD)). This approach requires
neither pressurization nor ultra-high-temperature operations, with
mild reaction conditions enabling effortless large-scale material pre-

LiCl@Ni
2Cl2(BTDD)_30 was chosen for the key structural character-
istics and theﬁnal device (Supplementary Figs. 3–5), because it enables
a synergistic balance of the high water adsorption capacity and fast
adsorption kinetics (details see the section “MOF-composite with
varying LiCl solution concentrations ” in the Supplementary
Information).

actual system formation
13,15,18,35. Thus, this composite strategy can
better enhance the applicability of AWH under conditions of low
irradiation and heat source temperatures.
Collectively, the ﬁeld tests validate that the MOF-composite sig-
niﬁcantly enhances solar-to-water generation across diverse climates.
The solar-driven AWH system operates by utilizing low-grade solar
thermal energy to desorb at a lower temperature, thereby achieving
the transformation of water vapor into liquid water. Consequently, the
improvement in water collection ef ﬁciency re ﬂects an overall
enhancement in energy ef ﬁciency. Speci ﬁcally, the ef ﬁciency
improvement reached 91% in Jinan under a continental climate, while
an average enhancement of approximately 50% was achieved in
medium-to-high humidity regions such as Shanghai and Jinan. These
results collectively underscore the signiﬁcant potential of synergizing
MOF-derived composite sorbents with advanced thermal management
in AWH devices.
Water quality analysis
The analysis of water quality indicates that, within the allowable pre-
cision range of testing instruments (details of testing instruments are
introduced in Supplementary Information: Details of water ion con-
centration analysis), the ion concentration of the proposed water
sample is identical to that of distilled water. No lithium ions or nickel
ions were detected, conﬁrming its suitability for direct consumption.
This result demonstrates that the collected water originates entirely
from the condensation of water vapor generated during the deso-
rption process on the condenser walls (as shown in Table 2). Further-
more, it conﬁrms the absence of salt leakage throughout the process.
Discussion
To improve the efﬁciency of the solar-driven AWH system, we propose
a strategy of introducing hygroscopic salts to construct composite
adsorbents, achieving enhanced dynamic adaptability and desorption
performance for MOF materials under varying humidity conditions.
The composite sorbent based on the selected Ni
2Cl2(BTDD) offers
multiple advantages. First, it signiﬁcantly reduces the required deso-
rption temperature at the system level. Moreover, the encapsulation of
LiCl within its porous structure promotes uniform dispersion of the
salt, thereby improving heat and mass transfer ef ﬁciency. Further-
more, this structure effectively prevents the aggregation, leakage, or
migration of LiCl under high-humidity conditions. The developed
composite achieves near-complete desorption at 60 °C and delivers
higher water uptake with increasing humidity, offering ideal properties
for broad environmental compatibility. The ﬁeld results in different
locations show that the proposed system with MOF-composite can
achieve high-performance water vapor production at the system level
with a driving temperature of only 60 °C and boost the water har-
vesting efﬁciency by up to 91% under the operating conditions in Jinan.
These experimental data indicate that by using the proposed
environment-adaptive strategy, the composite sorbent can better
adapt to the bene ﬁts brought by increased humidity and longer
moisture absorption time in the future.

Synthesis of LiCl@Ni2Cl2(BTDD)
We initially synthesized the MOF material Ni 2Cl2(BTDD) via a re ﬂux
synthesis method based on previously reported preparation proto-
cols, followed by drying the obtained product. The dried MOF powder
was then immersed in a 10/20/30/40 wt% LiCl (Anhydrous LiCl with
AR ≥ 99.0% was purchased from Shanghai Aladdin Biochemical Tech-
nology Co., Ltd.) solution for a 24-h impregnation treatment. Magnetic
stirring was applied during the impregnation process to ensure sufﬁ-
cient penetration of the LiCl solution into the MOF pores. The resulting
solution was then centrifuged at 1000 rpm (10,619 × g)t oa c h i e v e
preliminary solid-liquid separation, with the material precipitating at
the bottom of the centrifuge tubes. Centrifugation was performed for
10 min to ensure complete phase separation. The separated material
was subsequently dried at 80 °C to obtain the ﬁnal composite adsor-
bent LiCl@Ni
2Cl2(BTDD). For ease of description, we named the MOF
composite material immersed in a 10/20/30/40 wt% LiCl solution as
LiCl@Ni2Cl2(BTDD)_10/20/30/40, respectively.
