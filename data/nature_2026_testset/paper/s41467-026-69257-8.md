fundamental step in the activation of small molecules and homo-
geneous catalysis

acidity) of the metal center, particularly in cationic complexes of late
transition metals, has enabled the transformation of otherwise inert
substrates through nucleophilic attack. This electrophilic reactivity

Given their reactivity, there is considerable interest in developing
high-valent (>+2), cationic transition-metal complexes with strong
electrophilic character. However, these species often suffer from low

Despite this, structurally deﬁned Co

A relevant example by Ribas and coworkers invoked a macrocyclic-

In designing such complexes, the choice of counterion is critical.

coordinating, non-nucleophilic, and highly resistant to oxidation;
three properties that are intrinsically interlinked
