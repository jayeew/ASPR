chemistry, and the metal–nonmetal inverted half-sandwich structure (inverse
metallocene) was very recently discovered. However, additional instances are
required to propose the concept inverse metallocene and it remains uncertain
whether metal ﬁve-membered ring structures analogous to the cyclopenta-
dienyl anion structure exist. Hereinwe report the synthesis of a series of
palladium analogs Pd

(PPh3)(S-Adm)6 (Pd6), Pd5(PPh)(S-Adm)4[(Ph2P)2O] (Pd5–O), and Pd5(PPh)(S-
Adm)4[(Ph2P)2CH2]( P d5–C), identify their fundamental metal building block
named ﬁv e - m e m b e r e dP da r o m a t i cr i n g ,a n dr e v e a lt h e i rc o n j u g a t i o n‒photo-
thermy correlation. In particular, we report an average NIR-II photothermal

palladocenes in areas such as laser shielding, high-temperature degradation,
ignition, and temperature/light control. These results declare the research and
application start of a type of materials named inverse palladocenes.
The isolation and structural elucidation of ferrocene marked the
inception of contemporary organometallic chemistry 1– 4. Since this
milestone discovery, sandwich (or half-sandwich) metallocenes have
been extensively reported
5–8. The cyclopentadienyl anion (Cp, [C5H5]−)
serves as the fundamental building block for this type of material. It
comprises 6π electrons, adhering to the Hückel 4n + 2 rule of aroma-
ticity (Fig. 1a), and giving metallocenes unusual stability for practical
applications in biology, pharmaceuticals, materials, etc
9–14. However,
the extension of this nonmetallic Cp to analogous metal ﬁve-
membered rings, as well as the prospective metal aromaticity of the
latter, requires further investigation. Interestingly, we recently pro-
duced an inverse metallocene-like structure
15, in which the pentalene
(C8) anion was replaced by a structurally similar two-fused and con-
jugated ﬁve-membered palladium (Pd8) ring, and the metal cation in
metallocene was replaced by a nonmetal anion, as shown in Supple-
mentary Fig. 1. The revealed nonmetal-to-metal ring coordination, as
well as the aromatic Pd
8 cation, was not previously reported16– 19,w h i c h
might indicate the emergence of a type of material: inverse metallo-
cene. However, additional instances are required to validate a single
case report. Speci ﬁcally, it remains uncertain whether metal ﬁve-
membered ring structures analogous to the Cp structure exist, which
inspired this work.

We synthesized a series of inverse palladocene materials by
developing synthesis methods. We demonstrated that inverse metal-
locene or nonmetal-to-metallo-aromatic coordination is not a singular
instance and predicted the emergence of a new type of material that
obscures the border of metal and nonmetal bonding. Further, we
identiﬁed the fundamental metal building block of theﬁve-membered
Pd aromatic ring from the series of inverse palladocenes.
We introduced a new physical quantity, the nominal coplanar
bond length standard deviation (S
ncp), to evaluate the conjugation
degree of inverse palladocenes and revealed conjugation ‒photo-
thermy correlation: the photothermal conversion ef ﬁciencies of
inverse palladocenes increase with the conjugation degree increase.
We also found that the NIR absorption peak position and intensity
positively correlate with conjugation degree for the series of inverse
palladocenes. An interesting ﬁnding is that the photothermy perfor-
mance was dominated by the ﬁv e - m e m b e r e dP da r o m a t i cr i n g ,w i t h
the small ﬁve-membered ring achieving a remarkable PCE of 73.6%
under NIR-II 980 nm irradiation and an average PCE of 14.7% per metal
atom. This was much higher than that of other reported photothermy
materials, demonstrating the uniqueness of the ﬁve-membered Pd
aromatic ring. The photothermy performance of Pd
5‒C was main-
tained for at least 10 heating –cooling cycles after removing the
ligands, demonstrasting its photothermy stability. The outstanding
photothermy performance endows Pd
5‒C with a wide range of
potential applications, including laser shielding, high-temperature
degradation, ignition, temperature/light control, etc. Overall, our
research establishes a type of material with stability (aromaticity) and
promising photothermy performance, with enormous potential
applications. The extension of such material research and applications
is underway in our lab.

Computational details
The geometry optimizations of the Pd5‒C, Pd5‒O, Pd6,P d8‒S, and Pd8‒

basis sets were used for H, C, P, and S atoms, while relativistic effective
core potentials (RECP) with the associated valence basis set were
applied for Pd atom. NBO 7.0 package
65 was utilized to perform natural
population analysis (NPA) and compute natural atomic orbitals (NAOs)
for facilitating subsequent adaptive natural density partitioning
(AdNDP)
66 analyses. AdNDP analysis was calculated with Multiwfn
3.8 package67.
Atomic charge analyses, electron localization functions (ELF) 68

mical valence (EDA-NOCV)

calculations by using ADF 2019.304 package71. The all-electron polar-

and DZP Slater-type basis sets were adopted for other atoms
72.T h e

order regular approximation (ZORA)
73. Reduced density gradient

RDG curve was visualized by using Multiwfn 3.8 67 and VMD
1.9.3 software76. The isochemical shielding surfaces (ICSS) analysis was
also performed with Multiwfn 3.8 software and visualized by
VMD 1.9.3.

were carried out by using PBE
77 functional along with LANL2TZ ECP
