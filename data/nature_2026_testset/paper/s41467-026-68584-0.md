In this work, we develop a multiple oxidation atomic layer
deposition (MOALD) approach for high-quality ultrathin HfO
2 dielec-
trics at an 8-inch wafer scale. The deposition is performed at a rela-
tively low temperature of 200 °C, rendering it compatible not only
with commercial Si complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor
(CMOS) technology but also with emerging two-dimensional semi-
conductor-basedﬁeld-effect transistors (2D FETs). Notably, we report
the realization of the 1.3-nm-thick HfO
2 dielectric layer with a small
EOT of 2.5 Å. As-achieved HfO2 ﬁlms exhibit an exceedingly low leak-
age current below 10−6 A/cm2 at 1 V gate voltage and showcase a robust
breakdown electricﬁeld of ~22.3 MV/cm. The application of such a low-
EOT high-κ layer in cutting-edge 2D gate-ﬁrst and gate-last FETs based
on monolayer molybdenum disul ﬁde (MoS 2) is demonstrated to
evaluate its practicality and efﬁcacy in gate control. Remarkably, 100-
nm-channel devices feature an on-state current density of ~260µA/µm,
a high on/off ratio of ~10 8, and an average subthreshold slope (SS) of
~75 mV/dec at the source-drain bias of 0.5 V. We further fabricate
8-inch wafer-scale logic gates and memory devices such as inverters,
logic gates (e.g., AND, NAND, and NOR) and ﬁve-stage ring oscillators
(ROs) with correct functions. Such ultimately scaled EOT not only
transcends the goal of IRDS for 2037, but also holds substantial
implications for the forthcoming advanced semiconductor processes
towards the ångström era.

Methods
Synthesis of ultrathin HfO2 ﬁlm
First, we deposit 10 nm Au as the metal gate layer via E-beam eva-
poration on the cleaned 300 nm SiO2/Si substrate. Then, 0.5-nm-Ti is
deposited on metal gates as the seeding layer for ALD of HfO 2
dielectric layer. The ALD process is carried out in the Picosun R-200
Adv system. For the normal (one-step) ALD process, Tetra-
kis(ethylmethylamino)hafnium (TEMAH) and O
3 serve as Hf sources
and oxygen sources, respectively. The pulse (purge) time are 1.6 (25) s
for TEMAH and 1.6 (30) s for O
3. The carrier gas is pure N2 with a ﬂow
rate of 150 sccm for both TEMAH and O3. The TEMAH source is heated
up to 125 °C during the deposition. For the MOALD process, the
deposition condition for the Hf source remains the same, while the
oxygen sources are alternated between successive O
3 and O2 plasmas.
The pulse and purge times of O 3 and O 2 plasma are 1.6 and 25 s,
respectively, with the O2 plasma operating at a power of 1500 W. Post-
testing revealed ﬁlm physical thickness of 1.3, 2.6, and 3.9 nm were
achieved after 10, 20, and 30 cycles, respectively.
Sample characterizations
Raman and photoluminescence spectra are collected in Horiba Lab-
Ram HR Evolution with a laser excitation wavelength of 532 nm and a
power of 2 mW. Film thickness is measured by spectropic Mueller
matrix ellipsometer ME-mapping. Scanning electron microscope ima-
ges and cross-sectional STEM-HAADF images were obtained by Reg-
ulus 8100 and Thermo Fisher Spectra 300, respectively. AFM are
performed by Oxford Instruments Asylum Research Cypher S. X-ray
photoelectron spectroscopy measurements are conducted with the
Thermo Fisher ESCALAB XI+.
Device fabrications and measurements
MoS2 FETs and logic gates are fabricated via standard microfabrica-
tion processes. Fabrications involve UV lithography, reactive ion
etching and electron-beam evaporation to de ﬁne device structures,
etch MoS
2 and deposit metal gate or contact, respectively. An 8-inch
monolayer MoS 2 ﬁlm is transferred from sapphire to high- κ/metal
gate by the PMMA-assisted method. For contact metal, Au/Ti/Au (3/
2/30 nm) are deposited in vacuum at a base pressure of ~2 × 10
−7 torr
with a rate of 0.01 Å·s−1. All electrical measurements are performed in
the Lake Shore probe station under 10 −6 torr with a Keysight semi-
conductor analyzer B1500 equipped with high-resolution modules at
room temperature.
Simulations
A “melt-quench” process and classical molecular dynamics (MD) are
employed to generate initial amorphous structures41. Here, the initial
HfO2 amorphous systems are generated using this melt-quench
method with MD implemented in the LAMMPS software package 42.
The interatomic potential is considered as the charge-optimized many-
body potential (COMB)
43. The interatomic potential originally used a
core-shell model with ionic charge +4 for Hf and−2 for O, respectively.
