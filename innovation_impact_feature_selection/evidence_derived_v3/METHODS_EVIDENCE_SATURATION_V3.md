# Methods template: evidence-derived domains and T0 indicators

## Scope and temporal boundary

We mapped English journal articles, conference articles represented by the
OpenAlex article type, and reviews published no later than 28 July 2026. The
target constructs were article-level scientific novelty and potential
scholarly impact observable at publication time (`T0`). Post-publication
citations, attention, diffusion, and disruption were permitted only as
validation outcomes, never as predictor features. Restricting eligibility to
English evidence may underrepresent constructs and validation traditions from
non-English-speaking regions and is reported as a language/geographic
coverage limitation.

## Why the number of search domains was not predefined

The initial OpenAlex expression contained only three generic blocks:
scientific-paper objects, novelty/potential-impact targets, and
measurement/prediction/validation evidence roles. No earlier D01–D12 label
was included. The broad expression supplied an inventory denominator, not a
relevance-ranked sample or a final query set.

Source-linked English terms were collected from:

1. deterministic samples across publication eras and eligible work types;
2. oversamples for each generic target and evidence-role term;
3. high-precision probes generated by deduplicating metric/formula phrases
   from 53 development evidence papers;
4. the complete forward and backward OpenAlex citation neighbourhood of
   those development papers; and
5. later citation rounds from included reviews and indicator
   definition/application/validation papers.

AI produced a role-separated coding stream. Human 1 supplied the adopted
review stream used for comparison, and Human 2 adjudicated every retained
domain assignment and all disagreements.
The initial entries in the frozen H1 screening/term worksheets and H2
hidden-seed worksheets were generated as automated drafts. On 29 July 2026,
the project owner attested that the exact hashed files had been manually
reviewed and adopted, so they were imported as human-reviewed H1/H2 results.
Their source snapshots are explicitly labelled
`review_import_human_attested_automated_draft`; they are not represented as
originally manual or independently generated blind drafts. Any agreement
statistics involving this H1 batch therefore quantify concordance with the
adopted reviewed draft and are not used as evidence that draft generation was
independent of automation. Unreviewed automated-human files remained invalid.
The H1 term draft used a fixed 28-label provisional domain dictionary; these
labels were treated only as H1 proposals. They could not determine `K`
without H2 merge/split adjudication, direct source support, PRESS review, and
seed-recall validation. The H2 hidden-seed builder performed live route-count
checks but contained a predeclared eligible DOI list, so DOI identity,
route-set reconciliation, OpenAlex indexability, and recall were still
machine-checked after import.
Task generation refused to overwrite any nonblank human worksheet. Known
unreviewed trial files were quarantined, disclosed with hashes, and blocked
from import by both path and SHA-256.
Role-separated imports were enforced: Human-2 records were not accepted
until both primary records existed, and a primary record could not be
overwritten after adjudication.
Synonyms, abbreviations, historical names, morphological variants, and
parameter variants were mapped to canonical families without collapsing
constructs that differed in theoretical object, explanatory role, or T0
boundary. Search concept domains (`K`) therefore emerged from adjudicated
term families; they were not quotas and were not treated as model
dimensions. Prior v2 terms could supply synonyms, but neither a domain nor a
logical query family could be established without at least one directly
source-verified evidence term. Phrases inherited from the v2
`formula_authorization` field were explicitly marked as non-authorizing
development hints; an authorizing development-seed term had to be
re-extracted verbatim from a hydrated English title or abstract.

## Why the number of queries was not predefined

Within each adjudicated search domain, one logical query family was created
only for an independent construct/evidence relationship. Synonym-only
variants remained in a shared OR block. Exact semantic duplicates, verified
complete-result-set subsets lacking an independent construct role, and
zero-hit families were archived with reasons. Splits required solely by
OpenAlex syntax or request length inherited their parent logical-query ID.
Consequently, `Q` counts non-redundant logical semantics and `P` counts
provider-executable requests; neither count was chosen in advance.

For each frozen physical query, OpenAlex supplied a full inventory count and
a seeded deterministic review pool of up to 10,000 records. These formal
query pools were paged lazily as successive frozen rank slices were needed
and entered the same sequential review process as the broad and
citation strata. Pool members not reached before the preregistered stopping
rule remained in the auditable sampling frame and were not classified as
screened exclusions.

Human 2 reviewed every logical family with the PRESS checklist. Recall was
tested first against 53 development papers and then against an independently
assembled set of eligible English papers hidden from initial term
generation. The H2 hidden-seed search and seed worksheets logged the exact
review-search route and both citation-tracking directions, including every
eligible DOI found. These worksheets were part of the attested, manually
reviewed draft batch described above. The
distinct DOI counts were checked within each route and the route-level DOI
union had to exactly match the imported eligible English hidden-seed set.
All OpenAlex-indexable seeds had to be recalled. Eligible non-indexable
papers were added directly with documented provenance.
For API efficiency, indexability and query recall were evaluated in OR
batches of at most 40 DOI identities. Returned DOI identities were then
expanded back to a per-seed list of matching physical-query IDs, so batching
changed request count but not the recall criterion.
Later citation tracking used the same identity-preserving principle.
Deduplicated backward OpenAlex IDs already present in the local evidence
database were reused, and remaining IDs were retrieved in frozen batches of
at most 100. Forward citation searches used cursor-paged batches of at most
50 source IDs. Query definitions, cursor checkpoints, provider totals, hits,
and reconstructed source-target edges were retained. A free-credit-aware
scheduler rotated key slots per request and failed over without persisting
either key. Batching therefore reduced provider requests without changing
the eligible citation neighbourhood.

Crossref verified DOI-target identity, normalized title, publication year,
and type. A year discrepancy was retained as a date variant when DOI, title
similarity of at least 0.85, and type agreed, reflecting online-first versus
issue dates. Only DOI/title/type identity conflicts or provider errors entered
the Human-2 resolution queue. Stable DOI-endpoint 404 responses were
classified as `crossref_doi_not_found` instead of being repeatedly requested;
no automated metadata check excluded a record. Up to four metadata requests
ran concurrently, while the main thread persisted assessments in frozen
record order and committed every record individually, preserving
deterministic decisions and resumability.

## Why discovery stopped without downloading every broad hit

The broad inventory exceeded three million records, so an exhaustive
download would add cost and screening volume without making the stopping
claim scientifically sharper. We instead used deterministic, hash-ordered
sequential review. Each reviewed batch took the next frozen rank slice from
every active stratum. After screening and source-preserving extraction, the
database counted two endpoints:

- newly introduced non-redundant English canonical search-term families;
- newly introduced canonical indicator families.

Human 2 could freeze discovery only after at least three consecutive fully
reviewed rounds yielded zero for both endpoints, all mandatory
adjudications were complete, hidden/development seed recall passed, PRESS had
no unresolved issue, and citation tracking produced no new endpoint. Thus,
the stopping rule was evidence saturation rather than a target number of
records, domains, or queries.

Saturation was evaluated in two explicit phases. The first phase froze the
terminology needed to derive and validate `K/Q/P`. After the resulting formal
query pools were registered, the consecutive-zero counter reset and a second
phase continued until formal-search and citation evidence yielded three new
dual-zero rounds. An early terminology-phase freeze could therefore not be
reused to claim final indicator saturation.

## How candidate dimensions and final indicators were determined

Indicators were extracted before model dimensions. Reviews could identify
names but could not authorize formulas. Each final candidate required an
English primary or foundational full text, an exact formula location,
formula/parameter/unit/direction/missing-value specification, article-level
computability, and a maximum information time no later than `T0`.
OpenAlex locations explicitly marked open access were acquired automatically
when they exposed an HTTP(S) PDF. Downloads were size bounded, checked for a
PDF signature, saved atomically, hashed, and registered as candidate source
snapshots. This automation prepared the review evidence but did not determine
its language, formula, or eligibility.
Compact discovery records were refreshed for OA-location fields only after
final inclusion. Verified UTF-8/PDF evidence then had to contain the recorded
English evidence span after Unicode and whitespace normalization, and formula
locations had to identify a page, table, equation, appendix, or section.
Human 1 dispositioned every included source and extracted its indicator
mentions. Human 2 then independently reviewed every source disposition and
approved or excluded every retained mention with an explicit adjudication
note; a Human-1-only worksheet could not complete the indicator stage.
Human-1 source and mention records were stored before the Human-2 worksheet
could be generated. Human-2 imports were accepted only for existing Human-1
source and mention identities. Both normalized reviewer records and their
immutable input snapshots were retained; the adjudicated operational row did
not overwrite the primary-review evidence.
For every verified full-text mention, the lawful source URL, access/licence
statement, local evidence-file path, and computed SHA-256 were retained.
Those evidence files entered the frozen source manifest, so a changed or
missing version invalidated the audit rather than silently preserving a
formula claim.

After synonym and parameter-variant deduplication, AI and Human 1
independently coded each canonical indicator family for construct,
explanatory role, information source, T0 boundary, and bias risk. Human 2
adjudicated all retained mappings and dimension merge/split decisions.
Human 1's worksheet contained source evidence but no AI/H2 decisions. The
Human-2 worksheet was generated only after both primary codes existed and
showed them side by side. The local AI model digest, prompt hash, input hash,
and item-level failures were retained.
Candidate dimensions (`M`) therefore emerged from indicator constructs, not
from the search-domain taxonomy.

An indicator was retained only if it passed every preregistered hard gate:
scope, article-level computation, peer-reviewed original/foundational
evidence, reproducible formula, T0 compliance, no future information,
auditable data availability, bias safeguards, no fatal construct-validity
problem, no outcome-driven admission, data-quality checks, and
non-constancy. Passing data audits were tied to a frozen input manifest and
derivation artifact whose SHA-256 values were computed during import and
retained in the source manifest; self-reported hashes without the
corresponding files were not accepted. Within a redundant family, one
representative was selected by
the frozen ordering of preregistered priority, evidence strength, data
availability, stability, and deterministic feature ID. Priority was
mechanically determined from the frozen evidence-role order (original
definition, mathematical foundation, original application, validation,
review discovery), rather than assigned after model inspection. Evidence
strength used a closed vocabulary, and the normalized stability score
required a recorded source/audit statistic and normalization basis; absence
of quantitative stability evidence was coded zero. A predictive
dimension was retained only if at least one indicator survived and at least
two independent research teams supported its construct boundary. Team
independence was counted using an H2-reviewed normalized team identifier
supported by an author/affiliation span found in the frozen English full
text. Only H2-approved, full-text-verified mentions contributed teams, so
multiple papers and spelling variants from one team contributed only one
group and unchecked labels contributed none.
Opportunity variables, context controls, and sensitivity variables were
reported separately and did not inflate the predictive-dimension count.

The final `K/Q/P/M/D/F` values must be inserted from the frozen audit report;
interim AI proposals or pilot labels are not reportable final values.
