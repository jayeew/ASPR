# V4 primary AI high-recall discovery routing

For each deterministic discovery record, preserve it for independent H1 review
whenever its English title or abstract is usable. Exclude only records with an
explicit non-English language field or insufficient title-and-abstract metadata.
Do not infer final eligibility, derive a dimension, or approve an indicator.
Every retained record remains subject to blind H1 review and H2 adjudication.
