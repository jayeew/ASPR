**Question.** Can publication-time signals identify topics that subsequently
become active research frontiers?

**Backtests.** Training/scoring/validation are strictly ordered as
≤1999/2000–02/2003–06, ≤2004/2005–07/2008–11, and
≤2009/2010–12/2013–16.

The frozen temporal-OOF artifacts separate papers by publication year but
do not impose a five-year D5 label-maturity embargo. Accordingly, the main
figure calls these retrospective historical-window tests, not literal
real-time forecasts.

**Panels.** Registered time bands; forecast/realization topic landscape;
predicted-to-realized Top-10 bump chart; locked seed-topic cards; and paired
comparisons against K1, historical growth, prior popularity, and random
baselines.

**Boundary.** Window heterogeneity and negative results are retained. Current
frontier deployment is not retroactively presented as validated forecasting.
