# Fig.2 — Measurement governance for five angles and eight indicators

**Question.** Where do the indicators come from, why are they assigned to
five observation angles, and do they pass computability, stability,
non-redundancy, and construct-plausibility checks?

**Data.** The frozen 50-candidate v6.1 registry, publication-time feature
table, source/reference histories, and D5 component labels.

**Panels.** An outcome-blind stable-hash G−/G0/G+5 scene; candidate-screening
Sankey; evidence map; violin/triangular correlation diagnostic; coverage and
resampling gates with known-group effects; and field-year-standardized
indicator associations with the five D5 components and composite target.

**Boundary.** G+5 and D5 are validation outcomes only. They are never used to
choose the focal paper or to add/remove indicators.
