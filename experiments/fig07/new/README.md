**Question.** How do innovation profiles and later D5 diffusion differ across
locally well-covered Nature Portfolio venue families?

**Data.** Nature flagship, Nature Communications, Scientific Reports, and
covered Nature specialist titles. The score is the venue-excluded,
innovation-only temporal OOF prediction.

**Panels.** Innovation/D5 portfolio map; bootstrap rank distributions;
Top-1%/5% enrichment intervals; separate five-angle radar profiles; within-
venue innovation/D5 associations; and a covariate-imbalance audit for field,
year, reference volume, and team metadata.

**Boundary.** Science, Cell, and PNAS are absent because they are not in the
local cohort. Differences are descriptive selection/portfolio associations,
not effects caused by a journal. The frozen author and institution counts are
identical, so institution count is withheld and common support is not claimed.
