(a) Cohort, six method inputs, evaluation channels and material separation. (b) Historical core references, precision/recall, R×P matrix, paired differences and historical overclaims. (c) Five independent report-quality dimensions, quality space and differences, shared contribution coverage, assertion types and an ID-selected quotation case. Historical references and evaluations are source-assisted model judgments, not newly collected expert gold labels. Existing reviewer records are incomplete and manuscript-scope dependent. Graph neighborhoods and paper citation paths cannot directly establish novelty or antecedence. Missing retrieval or technically incomplete tasks remain explicit; no score substitutes for failure. All valid-information counts use within-paper semantic clusters. Each quality dimension stands alone, with no total novelty score.

Main contrast is Full−GEAR; all five comparators are exported. Proportions are computed within paper before macro averaging; micro averages and actual denominators accompany figures. Intervals use 10,000 shared paper-level bootstrap draws with seed 20260922. These intervals describe sampling variation, not judge correctness. Repeated evaluation and synthetic controls remain separate and never automatically rewrite the main study.

Case eligibility is fixed in code and selection follows paper ID, without selecting on Full gains. Figure text excerpts may be abbreviated for space; component JSON/CSV retains full original quotes, evidence, scopes and identities. No simulated observations are substituted for missing data.

Current repair protocol: the same 100 papers, shared claims and native graph observations are reused. Only GEAR, Graph and Full analyses/reports are repaired; Direct-A, EACL and ReviewGrounder generation outputs remain unchanged. Shared historical references and evaluations are recomputed against original reports and source text. Full alone has no report-length target; the other conditions retain their original target, so the contrast does not isolate fusion architecture from report budget. Fusion decisions retain a claim-level record of why branch findings were retained, merged, corrected, omitted or left unresolved. New information is distinguished from already mentioned information that becomes supported. All comparisons remain empirical; a Full advantage is not required.
